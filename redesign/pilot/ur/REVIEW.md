# Urdu content for the 12 batch-one names: review sheet

Prepared 22 Nov 2026. Files: `redesign/pilot/ur/<slug>.json` (one per name). Nothing here is on any page yet.

## The short version

- **What is ready for you to check:** 4 "Did you know" facts, 22 famous-bearer entries and 12 page fixes, all in Urdu, across the 12 names. Every fact is already on the English page; nothing new was researched.
- **The main defect this closes:** on the Urdu pages most famous bearers are written in English letters with no description (for example "Mansur al-Hallaj", "Fadwa Tuqan"). Each is now in Urdu script with the same one-line description the English page has. Three duplicated entries are removed (Khadijah on Hatice, Ghada Abdel Razek on Ghada, the Prophet Ayyub on Ayyub) and the duplicated Abbasid caliph on Mansur.
- **Spellings:** every person and place name written in Urdu script was checked today against an Urdu Wikipedia article title, or the Arabic Wikipedia title for the Arabic original. The source is beside each one. The spelling and wording choices that need your eye are listed under "Please decide".
- **What is not done:** whole sections the English pages have and the Urdu pages lack. About 4,543 English words across the 12 pages (parent kit, fuller story, written answers to the questions). Listed per name under `gaps` in each file and summarised at the end.
- **Not touched:** the name's own Urdu and Arabic spelling, and all Quran text, on every page.

## Please decide

1. **mansur**: «المغرب» is used for the country Morocco and «مراکش شہر» for Marrakesh, following Urdu Wikipedia and the existing page. Many Urdu readers call the country «مراکش»: confirm which you want.
2. **zayd**: «خروج» is used for "revolt" (the classical term). If you prefer plain «بغاوت», say so.
3. **osman**: «بیگ» for Bey follows Urdu Wikipedia; «بے» is also common in Urdu. Confirm.
4. **khansa**: Khansa bint Khidam: sources spell the father's name both «خدام» and «خذام». «خدام» is used because the English page says Khidam. Confirm.
5. **khansa**: Honorifics «حضرت … رضی اللہ عنہا» are added for both women as Companions, following how the site writes Zayd and Khadijah. The existing Urdu page writes al-Khansa without them («خنساء بنت عمرو: شاعرۂ اسلام»).
6. **hatice**: Nothing new is written for this page. Hatice Sultan is NOT added as a bearer in Urdu because the English page names her only in the story text, not in the bearer list.
7. **fadwa**: «فدویٰ طوقان» combines the page's own Urdu spelling of the name («فدویٰ») with the family name as the page text and Urdu Wikipedia (her brother's article) write it. Urdu Wikipedia titles her article «فدوا توقان»; that spelling looked wrong and was not used.
8. **ghada**: «ہیپٹاتھلون» is a transliteration of heptathlon; no Urdu Wikipedia article exists to check it against. Confirm or replace with a description («سات مقابلوں کا کھیل»).
9. **ghada**: «تمغا» follows Urdu Wikipedia usage; one existing Urdu page on the site writes «تمغہ».
10. **ghada**: The Urdu page text names «مصری لکھاری غادہ عبد العال» as a famous bearer; the English story names the Lebanese journalist Ghada Eid instead, and the bearer list has the actress Ghada Abdel Razek. Three different people: not changed, your call.
11. **luqman**: The existing Urdu page writes Luqman without «حضرت»; «حکیم لقمان» (the Urdu Wikipedia title) is used the same way.
12. **ayyub**: The origin label says «عربی» while the page text says the name comes from Hebrew: the same open decision as on the English page. Not changed.

## Not verified, left out

- **bayezid**: Bayezid Bistami: the clause "the Tayfuriya tariqa traces its name to him". No Urdu-script source for the spelling of Tayfuriya was found today (the Urdu Wikipedia article could not be fetched).

## Looks wrong on the Urdu page today (listed, not fixed)

Counts are for all Urdu name pages on the site (2,448), measured 22 Nov 2026, so you can see which are one-page slips and which are template faults.

### Template faults (same wording on many pages)

- «حاملہ» is used for a girl who bears the name («یہ نام اپنے حاملہ سے چاہتا ہے»). In everyday Urdu «حاملہ» means a pregnant woman. On 1,049 pages. Seen here on khansa, hatice, fadwa, ghada, kainat. *Most serious: it reads wrongly on every girl's page.*
- Famous bearers in English letters with no description. On 950 pages. On 69 pages the dates also keep English "b." or "c." (zayd, ghada, ahlam here). *The fix in this sheet covers 12 pages; the rest need the same treatment.*
- «ہجے» is used for syllables («۲ ہجے»). «ہجے» means spelling. On 2,050 pages. *A reader takes it as "2 spellings".*
- «… کا اصل کیا ہے» in the questions. «اصل» is feminine in Urdu («… کی اصل کیا ہے»). On all 2,448 pages. Also «اصل عربی ہے», «اصل ماخذ میں اصل درج نہیں ہے» (157 pages, bayezid here), which does not read as a sentence.
- Meaning line begins with a stray closing mark: «ابو یزید» کی …» is printed as «ابو یزید» کی» without its opening «. On 61 pages (bayezid, osman here).
- Section headings «حاملین» and «یہ نام کس نے اٹھایا» are word-for-word from English "bearers" and "who has carried this name". Natural Urdu would be «مشہور شخصیات» / «اس نام کی مشہور شخصیات».
- «انگریزی قرات» is spelled «قرات» while the same page spells the word «قراءت» elsewhere.
- Sections are numbered out of order on screen (حصہ ۲, ۵, then ۱). *Layout: agent A.*
