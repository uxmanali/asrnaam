Is Merima an Islamic name? Yes. Merima is an established name in Muslim use, Arabic in origin. Its settled meaning is: Bosnian form of Maryam. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and claim nothing that belongs to God alone, and this name meets both.

Meaning

Merima (مريمة) means: Bosnian form of Maryam. The exalted, the pure.

Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

م-ر-ي

The root letters of Merima are م-ر-ي. From this same root the classical Arabic dictionaries derive a whole family of words that share the underlying idea. In Ilm ul Huroof each letter of the root is read for its ontological quality; the sum of the root letters gives the field of meanings that the name is set inside.

See the letter reading above for how each letter carries its share of the whole.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Merima joins fire to earth, the will that decides with the patience that lasts. This is a name that asks its bearer to burn steadily rather than in bursts, and to plant what the fire has cleared. Running through the reading is Al-Mu'min, the Faithful, carried as a faith others can lean on, and beside it Al-Tawwab, the Relenting, adding the readiness to return and be returned to. A person named Merima plants what their fire has cleared.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

Merima is an Arabic name, drawn from the classical Arabic lexicographic tradition (Lisan al-Arab, al-Sihah, Taj al-Arus). The name carries the meaning: Bosnian form of Maryam. The exalted, the pure.

Every letter of Merima in the Arabic script has been read in the classical tradition of Ilm ul Huroof as an ontological principle: the letters do not merely spell the sound of the name, they carry the qualities the tradition ascribes to it. See the letter reading above.

In Islam, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "You will be called on the Day of Resurrection by your names and the names of your fathers, so give yourselves good names" (Abu Dawud). A well-chosen name is a lifelong invocation of the meaning it carries.
