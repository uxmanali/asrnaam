Tehreem

A reading of Tehreem: the meaning, the letters, the demand it makes on the one who carries it.

Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

ح-ر-م

ح-ر-م (to make sacred). This root is the linguistic-spiritual family of the name; other names sharing the same root carry a related weight.

Section 2 · Ilm ul Huroof

Read Letter by Letter

تَحْرِيم
Ta-light ت the multiplied surface.
Ha-breath ح the breath of enclosure.
Ra ر motion, mercy in movement.
Ya ی aspiration, reaching.
Meem م the maternal, water.

Ta manifests, Ha carries breath, Ra moves in mercy, Ya reaches, Meem is maternal. Sacred and honoured.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Tehreem weaves earth and water, the elements of steadiness and mercy, patience and feeling. The person is asked to be both root and river, a slow, sheltering presence. Running through the reading is Al-Tawwab, the Relenting, meaning the bearer is trusted with the readiness to return and be returned to, joined by Al-Mu'min, the Faithful, carried as a faith others can lean on. A person named Tehreem holds their people through seasons.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

تحریم Tehreem کا مطلب: حرمت والی، مکرم۔ ہر وہ چیز جو مقدس ہو۔

Tehreem, from the Arabic root ح-ر-م (haram, ihram, hurmah), names the sacred and honoured one. The name shares its root with the Haramayn (the two sanctuaries of Mecca and Medina) and with Surah At-Tahrim (Chapter 66) of the Quran. Set apart, honoured, protected.
