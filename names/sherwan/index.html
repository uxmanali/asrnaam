Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

Sherwan is a name of Kurdish origin in Muslim use. It does not carry a classical Arabic triliteral root; its letters in the Arabic script are still read for their qualities in Ilm ul Huroof, as the next section shows.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Sherwan joins fire to earth, the will that decides with the patience that lasts. This is a name that asks its bearer to burn steadily rather than in bursts, and to plant what the fire has cleared. Running through the reading is Al-Shakur, the Appreciative, carried as a way of noticing and honouring small kindnesses, and beside it Al-Nur, the Light, adding a light that steadies the room. A person named Sherwan tends to build things that outlast them.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

Sherwan is a Kurdish name, kept in use by Kurdish Muslim families across Iraq, Iran, Turkey, and Syria. The name carries the meaning: Lion-hearted. Kurdish.

Every letter of Sherwan in the Arabic script has been read in the classical tradition of Ilm ul Huroof as an ontological principle: the letters do not merely spell the sound of the name, they carry the qualities the tradition ascribes to it. See the letter reading above.

In Islam, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "You will be called on the Day of Resurrection by your names and the names of your fathers, so give yourselves good names" (Abu Dawud). A well-chosen name is a lifelong invocation of the meaning it carries.
