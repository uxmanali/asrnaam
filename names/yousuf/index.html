Is Yousuf an Islamic name? Yes. Yousuf is an established name in Muslim use. Its settled meaning is: Joseph. Its root appears in the Quran, and the name is in continuous use across the Muslim world. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and claim nothing that belongs to God alone, and this name meets both.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

From the Arabic يوسف (Yusuf), which classical philologists trace to the Hebrew Yosef (He will add, He will increase). Yousuf is the dominant South Asian transliteration, common in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, and the Gulf, where the long u is written 'ou' to mark its length.

The name belongs to the Prophet Yusuf (AS), whose entire story unfolds across a single surah of the Quran, Surah Yusuf, called by Allah the most beautiful of stories (ahsana al-qasas). Betrayed by his brothers, sold into slavery in Egypt, imprisoned, then raised to authority over the treasuries of the land, he is the paradigm of patient beauty under trial.

Yousuf is a spelling, not a different name. Underneath the 'ou' is the same Prophet whose beauty made women lose their senses and whose tears watered his father's blindness back to sight.

Yusuf (AS), the Prophet Joseph, has an entire surah of the Quran named for him: Surah Yusuf (12), 111 verses that Allah calls ahsana al-qasas, the most beautiful of stories.

The surah tells the whole arc of his life: the dream, the brothers' betrayal, the pit, the caravan, the house of the Egyptian minister, the wife's plot, the prison, the interpretation of the king's dream, and the reunion in Egypt where Yaqub's family bowed before him as his boyhood dream had foretold. Yusuf's phrase 'no reproach on you today' (12:92) to his brothers is the classical model of prophetic forgiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

About the name Yousuf

What does the name Yousuf mean?
Yousuf means: Joseph, the South Asian spelling widely used in Pakistan and India. It is a spelling variant of Yusuf, different English transliteration of the same Arabic name يوسف.
Is Yousuf the same as Yusuf?
Yes, Yousuf and Yusuf are different English spellings of the same Arabic name. They carry identical meaning and identical Islamic heritage.
Is Yousuf a good Islamic name?
Yes, Yousuf is an excellent Islamic name. It is a valid transliteration of Yusuf (يوسف), Joseph, the South Asian spelling widely used in Pakistan and India.
Which spelling is correct, Yousuf or Yusuf?
Both are correct. Arabic names can be spelled multiple ways in English because English does not have a standardised system for transliterating Arabic letters. The Arabic name يوسف is the same regardless of English spelling. Use whichever spelling your community and family prefers.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of Yousuf?
The Ilm ul Huroof reading is based on the Arabic letters of the name, not the English spelling. See the complete letter-by-letter spiritual reading on the Yusuf page.
