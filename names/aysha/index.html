Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

ع-ي-ش

ع-ي-ش, From ʿ-y-sh, to live, the same root as ʿaysh (life). ʿĀʾisha is the active participle: 'she who lives, full of life'.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Aysha joins fire to earth, the will that decides with the patience that lasts. This is a name that asks its bearer to burn steadily rather than in bursts, and to plant what the fire has cleared. Running through the reading is Al-Alim, the All-Knowing, carried as a knowing that predates study, and beside it Al-Tawwab, the Relenting, adding the readiness to return and be returned to. A person named Aysha tends to build things that outlast them.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

Rooted in the Arabic root ع-ي-ش (to live, to be full of life), Aysha is a simplified diaspora spelling of the classical عائشة (Aisha), Mother of the Believers. The letter alif that follows the ayn in the canonical spelling is dropped in this phonetic English rendering, common in Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, and the UK-Pakistani diaspora.

The spelling changes, the meaning does not. Aysha still names the girl the Prophet ﷺ called Humayra (the little red one), the young companion who narrated over two thousand ahadith and shaped the Muslim community's religious life after his passing.

Families who choose Aysha are choosing the sound of the name their tongue is comfortable with while keeping the woman it honours whole.

Frequently Asked Questions

About the name Aysha

What does the name Aysha mean?
Aysha means: Living, happy, a simplified spelling used worldwide. It is a spelling variant of Aisha, different English transliteration of the same Arabic name عائشة.
Is Aysha the same as Aisha?
Yes, Aysha and Aisha are different English spellings of the same Arabic name. They carry identical meaning and identical Islamic heritage.
Is Aysha a good Islamic name?
Yes, Aysha is an excellent Islamic name. It is a valid transliteration of Aisha (عائشة), Living, happy, a simplified spelling used worldwide.
Which spelling is correct, Aysha or Aisha?
Both are correct. Arabic names can be spelled multiple ways in English because English does not have a standardised system for transliterating Arabic letters. The Arabic name عائشة is the same regardless of English spelling. Use whichever spelling your community and family prefers.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of Aysha?
The Ilm ul Huroof reading is based on the Arabic letters of the name, not the English spelling. See the complete letter-by-letter spiritual reading on the Aisha page.
