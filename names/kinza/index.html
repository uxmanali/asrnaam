Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

ك-ن-ز

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Kinza holds earth and air in even measure, grounded body and quick mind. The letters ask their bearer to think without floating away, and to build without slowing down. Weaving through the reading is Al-Karim, the Generous, offering the bearer an open-handed generosity of spirit. A person named Kinza tends to be trusted with plans and with hands.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

کنزہ Kinza کا مطلب: خزانہ، قیمتی نگہداشت شدہ چیز۔

Kinza names the treasure, the precious thing kept safe. Al-Kanz al-Makhfi ('the hidden treasure') is a famous Sufi phrase drawn from a Hadith Qudsi in which Allah says: 'I was a hidden treasure, and I loved to be known, so I created the creation to be known.' To name a daughter Kinza places her in that resonance.
