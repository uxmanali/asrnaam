Is Khwaja an Islamic name? Yes. Khwaja is an established name in Muslim use. Its settled meaning is: Master, lord, honoured teacher; Persian. It was carried by Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti.. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and claim nothing that belongs to God alone, and this name meets both.

Khwaja Name Meaning in Islam

A reading of Khwaja: the meaning, the letters, the demand it makes on the one who carries it.

Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

From the Persian khwaja: master, lord, teacher. This root is the linguistic-spiritual family of the name; other names sharing the same root carry a related weight.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Khwaja's letters draw from many elements at once, a full palette, not a single note. This is a name that asks its bearer to hold contradictions gracefully: warmth and steadiness, movement and depth. Running through the reading is Al-Khabir, the Aware, carried by a person as an aware, quietly informed presence. A person named Khwaja carries a full weather system inside a single quiet chest.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

خواجہ Khwaja کا مطلب: استاد، شیخ، مرشد۔ خصوصاً چشتی صوفی سلسلے میں اہم اصطلاح۔

Khwaja is a Persian honorific title meaning "master, lord, teacher, honoured elder." In the Chishti Sufi tradition, "Khwaja" is the title borne by the great masters: Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti of Ajmer, Khwaja Nizamuddin Auliya of Delhi, Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiar Kaki of Delhi. When given as a personal name, Khwaja carries the weight of that Sufi lineage: a parent placing their son under the aegis of the great masters.

Section 5 · Bearers

Who Has Carried This Name

  • Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti. 1141–1236. Founder of the Chishti Sufi order in India.
  • Khwaja Nizamuddin Auliya. 1238–1325. Great Sufi master of Delhi.
