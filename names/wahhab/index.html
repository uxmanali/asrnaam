Where the Name Comes From

و-ه-ب

Waw و the connecting conjunction.
Ha-breath ه presence, the light "he".
Alif ا the vertical axis of being.

Waw connects, Ha carries presence, Alif grounds in origin, Ba contains the gift. A name of connection, presence, origin, and vessel: the divine giving that shapes the vessel that receives.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Wahhab joins fire to earth, the will that decides with the patience that lasts. This is a name that asks its bearer to burn steadily rather than in bursts, and to plant what the fire has cleared. Running through the reading is Al-Wadud, the Loving, carried as a steady, connective love, and beside it Al-Batin, the Interior, adding an interior life that runs deeper than what is shown. A person named Wahhab plants what their fire has cleared.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

وہاب Wahhab کا مطلب: کثرت سے دینے والا، بلا معاوضہ عطا کرنے والا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ کا صفاتی نام "الوہّاب"۔

Wahhab, from the Arabic root و-ه-ب (w-h-b, "to give freely"), is one of the 99 divine names of Allah: Al-Wahhab, "The Bestower, The Great Giver." The intensifier form (fa''aal) indicates one who gives repeatedly, unstintingly, without expectation of return. When used as a human name, it is customarily preceded by "Abd," giving "Abdul Wahhab" (servant of the Bestower), following the classical rule for divine attributes.
