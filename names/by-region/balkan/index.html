How names work in Bosnia, Albania and Kosovo

Five centuries of Ottoman rule left European Muslim communities with Arabic names in Slavic and Albanian mouths. Bosnian naming keeps the Arabic meaning and Slavic sound: Mirela, Amela, Emir. The -ela and -eta endings are the local hand at work on borrowed material.

Questions about Balkan Muslim names

How many Balkan Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 22 used in Bosnia, Albania and Kosovo, 7 for boys and 15 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are Balkan names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of Bosnia, Albania and Kosovo and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which Balkan Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Mirela, Elvira and Adis lead. The settled meaning of Mirela is: Admirable, Bosnian Muslim girls name.
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
