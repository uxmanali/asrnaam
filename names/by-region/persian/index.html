How names work in Iran and the Persianate world

Persian gave Muslim naming its poetry. Where Arabic names tend to state a quality, Persian names tend to make an image: Gulnar is pomegranate flower, Mahtab is moonlight, Shahnaz is the pride of a king. Persian also lent Urdu, Turkish and much of Central Asia the letters پ چ ژ گ, for sounds Arabic does not have.

Questions about Persian Muslim names

How many Persian Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 73 used in Iran and the Persianate world, 27 for boys and 46 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are Persian names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of Iran and the Persianate world and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which Persian Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Cyrus, Payam and Elnaz lead. The settled meaning of Cyrus is: Cyrus, Dhul-Qarnayn in some traditions, the just Persian king.
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
