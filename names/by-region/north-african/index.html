How names work in the Maghreb

Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia write their names in Arabic and often spell them in French, which is why Abdelaziz and Abd al-Aziz are the same name and why a North African family may write Youssef where a Gulf family writes Yusuf. The Maghreb also carries a Berber substrate older than the Arabic, and names move in both directions across it.

Questions about North African Muslim names

How many North African Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 191 used in the Maghreb, 95 for boys and 96 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are North African names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of the Maghreb and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which North African Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Anas, Seraj and Chamsi lead. The settled meaning of Anas is: Affection, friendliness, intimacy.
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
