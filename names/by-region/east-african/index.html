How names work in Somalia, Kenya, Tanzania and Zanzibar

The Swahili coast has been Muslim and trading with Arabia for a thousand years, and Swahili itself is a Bantu language carrying a great deal of Arabic. Names travel easily along that route, and Somali naming keeps the Arabic close while coastal Swahili naming softens it.

Questions about East African Muslim names

How many East African Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 12 used in Somalia, Kenya, Tanzania and Zanzibar, 1 for boys and 11 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are East African names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of Somalia, Kenya, Tanzania and Zanzibar and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which East African Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Zubeda, Fatuma and Rahel lead. The settled meaning of Zubeda is: Zubaidah, East African Swahili form.
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
