How names work in Senegal, Mali, Nigeria and the Sahel

Islam reached West Africa across the Sahara, and the names came with it and then changed shape in Wolof, Fula, Mandinka and Hausa mouths. Muhammad becomes Mamadou. Uthman becomes Ousmane. Yusuf becomes Youssoupha. These are not corruptions but the ordinary work a living language does on a borrowed word, and the meaning survives the journey.

Questions about West African Muslim names

How many West African Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 84 used in Senegal, Mali, Nigeria and the Sahel, 49 for boys and 35 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are West African names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of Senegal, Mali, Nigeria and the Sahel and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which West African Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Idris, Hauwa and Yaqub lead. The settled meaning of Idris is: The prophet Idris (often identified with Enoch).
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
