How names work in the Arabic-speaking world

These are the names the rest of the Muslim world borrowed from. Arabic naming is built on the triliteral root: three consonants carrying a field of meaning, from which the language derives every word that belongs to it. K-R-M gives Karim, generous, and karam, the generosity itself, and akram, most generous. This is why an Arabic name can be read rather than merely looked up, and why the same name arrives in Lahore, Istanbul and Dakar carrying its meaning intact.
