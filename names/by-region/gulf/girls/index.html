What marks a gulf Muslim name

Gulf naming stays closer to classical Arabic than almost anywhere else, and revives names from the earliest generations that fell out of use elsewhere. Tribal and lineage names carry weight here that they no longer carry in the Levant or the Maghreb.

A name appearing on this page is not exclusive to the region. Most Muslim names travel, and the same root turns up under different spellings from Dakar to Dhaka. What the region tells you is where the name is at home: whose grandparents carried it, and which spelling a registrar there would expect.

Questions people ask

What are popular gulf Muslim girl names?
AsrNaam lists 62 of them, each researched against the classical Arabic lexicons. The list above is alphabetical rather than ranked, because popularity data for gulf naming is patchy and a ranking built on guesswork is worse than none.
Are gulf names Islamic names?
Origin and permissibility are different questions. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and not claim for a person what belongs to God alone. A name can be Persian, Turkic or Wolof in origin and meet both conditions, and many on this page do.
How is the Ilm ul Huroof reading produced?
Each Arabic letter of the name is read against the divine name and element the classical Science of Letters attaches to it. The full method and its sources are set out on the Ilm ul Huroof page.
