How names work in the Arabian Gulf

The Gulf keeps classical Arabic naming closest to its source, and adds a strong tribal register: names that mark lineage and standing as much as meaning. Modern Gulf naming is also quietly inventive, and a good deal of what trends across the Arab world now starts here.

Questions about Gulf Muslim names

How many Gulf Muslim names are there?
AsrNaam lists 89 used in the Arabian Gulf, 27 for boys and 62 for girls. Naming traditions overlap rather than divide, so many of these are used well beyond the region too.
Are Gulf names Arabic names?
Not all of them, and the distinction matters. A name is Islamic through its meaning, not through the language it comes from. Some of the names here are Arabic and travelled; others come from the languages of the Arabian Gulf and were carried into Muslim naming locally. Both are on this page, each with its origin stated.
Which Gulf Muslim name is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Nawaf, Qaboos and Shahad lead. The settled meaning of Nawaf is: High/tall.
Do these names have an Urdu spelling?
Yes. Every name on this site carries its Arabic spelling and its Urdu spelling separately, because Urdu writes ک ی ہ where Arabic writes ك ي ه. You can also browse the whole library by Urdu letter.
