What marks a bosnian name

Bosnian Muslim naming survived five centuries of Ottoman rule and a century of pressure afterwards, and what came through is Arabic and Turkish naming stock carried in a Slavic sound world. Emina, Amina, Adnan, Kenan.

The names are usually shortened and softened relative to their Arabic originals, and they take Slavic endings without difficulty. That adaptation is what allowed them to persist through periods when carrying an obviously Muslim name was dangerous.

A name is listed here only where its own research note records Bosnian use.

Questions people ask

What are common Bosnian Muslim baby names?
AsrNaam lists 23, alphabetically rather than by rank. Reliable bosnian birth-registration data is not public at the granularity a ranking would need, and a chart built on guesswork is worse than none.
Are Bosnian Muslim names Arabic?
Most are Arabic or Turkish in origin and Bosnian in form. The sound was adapted to Slavic phonology over centuries, which is why Amina, Emina and Mina all appear in the same family tree.
Does a Bosnian name have to be Arabic to be Islamic?
No. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and not claim for a person what belongs to God alone. A name can be Amazigh, Turkic, Persian or Malay in origin and meet both conditions.
