Arabic girl names beginning with N

The English letter N has no Arabic counterpart of its own. Every name on this page is written in Arabic beginning with Nun, and it is that letter, not the Latin N, that carries the reading in the Science of Letters.

Islamic girl names beginning with N

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

One of them appears in the Quran, or is borne by a figure named in it.

Questions about Muslim names starting with N

How many Muslim girl names start with N?
AsrNaam lists 86 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter N accounts for 131 researched names, 45 for boys and 86 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does N correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Nun (ن), so the reading of all 86 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin N is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim girl name starting with N is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Nihal, Noor ul Ain and Nadin lead the letter N. The settled meaning of Nihal is: Nihal, Saplings, Turkish/Arab girls. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with N?
Yes, one: Noor. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with N?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
