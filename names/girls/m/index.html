Questions about Muslim names starting with M

How many Muslim girl names start with M?
AsrNaam lists 90 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter M accounts for 196 researched names, 106 for boys and 90 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does M correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Meem (م), so the reading of all 90 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin M is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim girl name starting with M is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Maryam, Maham and Mirna lead the letter M. The settled meaning of Maryam is: The mother of Jesus, preserved in the Quran (Sūrat Maryam, 19). Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with M?
Yes, one: Maryam. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with M?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
