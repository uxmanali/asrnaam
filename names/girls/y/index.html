Islamic girl names beginning with Y

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

None of the names starting with this letter are Quranic in the strict sense, but each one has been checked for meaning and for the rule against divine-only titles before it was listed.

Questions about Muslim names starting with Y

How many Muslim girl names start with Y?
AsrNaam lists 19 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter Y accounts for 50 researched names, 31 for boys and 19 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does Y correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Ya (ي), so the reading of all 19 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin Y is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim girl name starting with Y is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Yumna, Yalina and Yusaira lead the letter Y. The settled meaning of Yumna is: Blessed, fortunate, the right-handed one, the woman of divine blessing. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are all names starting with Y Islamic names?
Not automatically. A name becomes Islamic through its meaning and through the absence of any claim on what belongs to God alone, not through its spelling or its first letter. Every name listed here has been checked on both counts.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with Y?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
