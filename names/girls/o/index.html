Arabic girl names beginning with O

The English letter O has no single Arabic counterpart. The 18 names on this page open with 3 different Arabic letters, and in the Science of Letters each of those carries a different quality, element and divine name. Two names that both look like O in English can begin very differently once they are written in Arabic.

  • أHamza opens 11 of them, among them Oumayma, Oumaima, Oumou.
  • عAin opens 3 of them, among them Ola, Olaa, Oraib.
  • وWaw opens 3 of them, among them Ouassila, Ouided, Ouissam.

Islamic girl names beginning with O

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

None of the names starting with this letter are Quranic in the strict sense, but each one has been checked for meaning and for the rule against divine-only titles before it was listed.

Questions about Muslim names starting with O

How many Muslim girl names start with O?
AsrNaam lists 18 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter O accounts for 46 researched names, 28 for boys and 18 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does O correspond to?
There is no single answer, which is what makes this letter interesting. Most often O stands for Hamza (أ), which opens 11 of these names. It can also stand for Ain and Waw. Each of those Arabic letters has its own element and divine name in the Science of Letters, so two names that look alike in English can read very differently.
Which Muslim girl name starting with O is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Oumayma, Oumaima and Oumou lead the letter O. The settled meaning of Oumayma is: Umayma, North African girls name. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are all names starting with O Islamic names?
Not automatically. A name becomes Islamic through its meaning and through the absence of any claim on what belongs to God alone, not through its spelling or its first letter. Every name listed here has been checked on both counts.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with O?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
