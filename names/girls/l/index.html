Islamic girl names beginning with L

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

Questions about Muslim names starting with L

How many Muslim girl names start with L?
AsrNaam lists 38 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter L accounts for 58 researched names, 20 for boys and 38 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does L correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Lam (ل), so the reading of all 38 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin L is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim girl name starting with L is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Lamia, Liyana and Lara lead the letter L. The settled meaning of Lamia is: Shining dark. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with L?
Yes, one: Laraib. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with L?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
