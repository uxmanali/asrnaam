Arabic girl names beginning with T

The English letter T has no single Arabic counterpart. The 39 names on this page open with 3 different Arabic letters, and in the Science of Letters each of those carries a different quality, element and divine name. Two names that both look like T in English can begin very differently once they are written in Arabic.

  • تTa opens 26 of them, among them Taleen, Taqwa, Taskeen.
  • طTa (heavy) opens 10 of them, among them Tayyaba, Tooba, Tahira.
  • ثTha opens 2 of them, among them Thana, Thuraya.

Each letter above links to its full reading: sound, abjad value, element, and the divine name it is associated with. See all twenty-eight letters.

Islamic girl names beginning with T

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

One of them appears in the Quran, or is borne by a figure named in it.

Questions about Muslim names starting with T

How many Muslim girl names start with T?
AsrNaam lists 39 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter T accounts for 82 researched names, 43 for boys and 39 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does T correspond to?
There is no single answer, which is what makes this letter interesting. Most often T stands for Ta (ت), which opens 26 of these names. It can also stand for Ta (heavy) and Tha. Each of those Arabic letters has its own element and divine name in the Science of Letters, so two names that look alike in English can read very differently.
Which Muslim girl name starting with T is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Taleen, Tayyaba and Taqwa lead the letter T. The settled meaning of Taleen is: Talin, Armenian/Syrian girls name. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with T?
Yes, one: Tasnim. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with T?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
