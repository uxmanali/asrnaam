Tahmina

A reading of Tahmina: the meaning, the letters, the demand it makes on the one who carries it.

Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

From the Shahnameh; Tahmina was the mother of Sohrab. This root is the linguistic-spiritual family of the name; other names sharing the same root carry a related weight.

Section 2 · Ilm ul Huroof

Read Letter by Letter

تTaAl-Tawwab · The RelentingEarthمMeemAl-Mu'min · The FaithfulFireیYaAl-Qayyum · The Self-SustainingEarthنNunAl-Nur · The LightEarth
تہمینہ
Ta-heavy ط manifestation, the appearance-becoming-real.
Ha-breath ه presence, the light "he".
Meem م the maternal, water.
Ya ی aspiration, reaching.
Nun ن the inkwell, hidden treasure.
Ha-light ه presence.

Ta manifests, Ha carries presence, Meem is maternal, Ya reaches, Nun closes into hidden treasure. A name of manifest presence, maternal aspiration, and preserved depth.

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Tahmina is grounded in earth, the element of stability, patience, and quiet holding. The letters ask their bearer to be reliable rather than showy, to keep the household of the heart in order. Stitched through this reading is Al-Tawwab, the Relenting, meaning the person carries the readiness to return and be returned to. A person named Tahmina builds ground under other people's lives.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

تہمینہ Tahmina کا مطلب: شاہنامہ فردوسی کی رومانوی کردار، رستم کی محبوبہ اور سہراب کی والدہ۔

Tahmina is a Persian name traced to Ferdowsi's Shahnameh (The Book of Kings). In that epic, Tahmina is the princess of Samangan, beloved of the great hero Rustam, and mother of Sohrab. The name carries the weight of that legend: a woman of noble bearing, of tragic love, of a mother whose son becomes his father's equal and unknown adversary. In Pakistani and Iranian Muslim families, Tahmina is a name given with deep cultural resonance.
