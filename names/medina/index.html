Is Medina an Islamic name? Yes. Medina is an established name in Muslim use. Its settled meaning is: The radiant city of the Prophet; Bosniak and Chechen girl name. It was carried by Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah.. Islamic tradition asks that a name carry a good meaning and claim nothing that belongs to God alone, and this name meets both.

Medina Name Meaning in Islam

A reading of Medina: the meaning, the letters, the demand it makes on the one who carries it.

Section 1 · Arabic Root

Where the Name Comes From

Section 3 · Synthesis

What This Reading Says About You

Medina is grounded in earth, the element of stability, patience, and quiet holding. The letters ask their bearer to be reliable rather than showy, to keep the household of the heart in order. Stitched through this reading is Al-Mu'min, the Faithful, meaning the person carries a faith others can lean on. A person named Medina is the household others rest against without noticing they are resting.

Section 4 · What Families Say

What People Usually Mean by This Name

مدینہ Medina کا مطلب: مدینہ منورہ، نبی کریم ﷺ کا شہر۔ بوسنیائی اور چیچن مسلم اسم۔

Medina names the daughter after al-Madinah al-Munawwarah ("the Illuminated City"), the city of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. After Mecca it is the second-holiest city in Islam, where the Prophet built the first mosque and is buried in his Rawdah. Both Bosniak and Chechen Muslim families give the name Medina to daughters as a form of du'a, may she carry the light of the Prophet's city with her.
