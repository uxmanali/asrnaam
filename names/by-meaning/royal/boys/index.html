What Muslim names meaning royal have in common

Malik, king, comes from a root meaning to own. In the Quran the word is used carefully: God is Malik al-Mulk, owner of all ownership, and human kingship is always borrowed. This is why names like Malik are given but Malik al-Mulk is not, and why the tradition forbids Shahanshah, king of kings. Amir is command rather than ownership, and sits more easily.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • م-ل-كmulk, kingship, ownership and dominion in one root.
  • أ-م-رamr, command, from which Amir.
  • س-ل-طsultan, authority, power to decide.

Questions about Muslim names meaning royal

How many Muslim boy names mean royal?
AsrNaam lists 71. Across boys and girls together, 102 researched names carry this meaning, 71 for boys and 31 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for royal?
Mulk, from the root م-ل-ك: kingship, ownership and dominion in one root. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim boy name meaning royal is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Shahrukh, Cyrus and Kian lead this theme. The settled meaning of Shahrukh is: King-face, Iranian/Pakistani boys name.
Are there Quranic names meaning royal?
Yes. 1 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Sulaiman. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning royal say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Sheen (ش), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
