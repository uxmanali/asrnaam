What Muslim names meaning love have in common

The Arabic for love is not one word. Hubb shares its root with habba, a seed, and carries the sense of something that takes hold and grows. Wudd is love that has settled into constancy, which is why al-Wadud is a divine name and hubb is not. Ishq is love that overwhelms, and the tradition is careful with it. Names drawn from these roots are not interchangeable.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • و-د-دwudd, settled, steady affection, and al-Wadud, the Loving.
  • ح-ب-بhubb, love as attachment, from the same root as a seed.
  • ع-ش-قishq, love as overwhelming, the word the Sufi poets took.

Questions about Muslim names meaning love

How many Muslim girl names mean love?
AsrNaam lists 27. Across boys and girls together, 39 researched names carry this meaning, 12 for boys and 27 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for love?
Wudd, from the root و-د-د: settled, steady affection, and al-Wadud, the Loving. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
