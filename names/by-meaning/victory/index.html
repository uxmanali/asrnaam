What Muslim names meaning victory have in common

Nasr is not victory won but victory granted: the root means to aid, and the Quran consistently makes God the one who gives it. Fath is the word for conquest, and it means opening, a door rather than a defeat. Fawz is success, and the Quran repeatedly calls paradise al-fawz al-azim, the great success, which quietly reframes what winning is. Names here carry that theology.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ن-ص-رnasr, victory given as aid, not victory taken.
  • ف-ت-حfath, opening, the word used for conquest.
  • ف-و-زfawz, success, and the great fawz is paradise.

Arabic baby names meaning victory

Every name here is an Arabic or Arabic-script name, and in Ilm ul Huroof the reading starts from the letter it opens with, not from the English transliteration. These are the letters that open the names in this theme.

  • فFa opens 19 of them, among them Faiza, Fawaz, Fathi.
  • ظDha opens 4 of them, among them Dhafir, Zafar, Zafira.
  • غGhayn opens 3 of them, among them Ghalib, Ghanem, Ghanim.
  • نNun opens 3 of them, among them Nasser, Nasr, Nasri.
  • تTa opens 3 of them, among them Taimoor, Taufiq, Tawfiq.

Each letter links to its element, abjad value and divine name. All twenty-eight letters.

Questions about Muslim names meaning victory

How many Muslim baby names mean victory?
AsrNaam lists 40. Across boys and girls together, 40 researched names carry this meaning, 31 for boys and 9 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for victory?
Nasr, from the root ن-ص-ر: victory given as aid, not victory taken. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim baby name meaning victory is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Kamran, Taimoor and Faiza lead this theme. The settled meaning of Kamran is: Successful, fortunate, Iranian/Pakistani.
Are there Quranic names meaning victory?
Yes. 1 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Nasr. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning victory say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Fa (ف), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
