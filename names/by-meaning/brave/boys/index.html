What Muslim names meaning brave have in common

The lion does most of the work here. Arabic has scores of words for lion and Muslim naming uses many of them: Asad, Haidar, Laith, Ghadanfar, Usama, each with a different shade. Shujaa is courage proper, a disposition rather than an animal. The tradition treats courage as the mean between recklessness and cowardice, so a name in this family is not a claim to fearlessness.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ش-ج-عshajaah, courage as a settled disposition.
  • أ-س-دasad, lion, the standing image of courage.
  • ب-س-لbasala, valour, and also what is forbidden, from fearlessness.

Questions about Muslim names meaning brave

How many Muslim boy names mean brave?
AsrNaam lists 66. Across boys and girls together, 71 researched names carry this meaning, 66 for boys and 5 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for brave?
Shajaah, from the root ش-ج-ع: courage as a settled disposition. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim boy name meaning brave is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Zaviyar, Zulfiqar and Hamza lead this theme. The settled meaning of Zaviyar is: Brave and noble, the number one trending Muslim boy name in Pakistan 2025-2026.
Are there Quranic names meaning brave?
Yes. 1 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Hamza. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning brave say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Ha (ح), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
