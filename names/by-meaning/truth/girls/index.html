What Muslim names meaning truth have in common

Arabic has two truths. Sidq is truthfulness in speech, the match between what is said and what is so, and Abu Bakr earned as-Siddiq for it. Haqq is truth as reality itself, and it is also the word for a right or a due, so al-Haqq is a divine name meaning at once the Real and the one to whom everything is owed. Names here take one road or the other.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ص-د-قsidq, truth as the match between word and reality.
  • ح-ق-قhaqq, truth as what is real and what is due.
  • أ-م-نamanah, trustworthiness, keeping what is entrusted.

Questions about Muslim names meaning truth

How many Muslim girl names mean truth?
AsrNaam lists 17. Across boys and girls together, 36 researched names carry this meaning, 19 for boys and 17 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for truth?
Sidq, from the root ص-د-ق: truth as the match between word and reality. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim girl name meaning truth is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Khadija, Afifa and Hanifa lead this theme. The settled meaning of Khadija is: Premature child.
Are there Quranic names meaning truth?
Yes. 4 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Aminah, Ikhlas and Khadija. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning truth say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Hamza (أ), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
