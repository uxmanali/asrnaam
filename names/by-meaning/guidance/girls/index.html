What Muslim names meaning guidance have in common

Huda, guidance, comes from a root that also gives hadiyyah, a gift. Guidance in this tradition is something handed over rather than achieved, which is why the opening chapter of the Quran asks for it as a request rather than claiming it. Rushd is different: the soundness of judgment that comes with maturity, and Rashid means one who has it.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ه-د-يhuda, guidance, from a root that also means to give a gift.
  • ر-ش-دrushd, right direction, maturity of judgment.

Questions about Muslim names meaning guidance

How many Muslim girl names mean guidance?
AsrNaam lists 16. Across boys and girls together, 28 researched names carry this meaning, 12 for boys and 16 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for guidance?
Huda, from the root ه-د-ي: guidance, from a root that also means to give a gift. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim girl name meaning guidance is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Hadiya and Nurhidayah lead this theme. The settled meaning of Hadiya is: Gift, guide, the divine gift and the one who guides to what is right.
Are there Quranic names meaning guidance?
Yes. 2 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Hidayah and Huda. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning guidance say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Ha (light) (ه), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
