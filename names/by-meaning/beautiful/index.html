What Muslim names meaning beautiful have in common

Arabic will not let beauty separate from goodness. Husn means both, and ihsan, from the same root, is the highest station of the religion: to worship as though you see Him. Jamal is beauty as completeness and proportion, and al-Jamil is a name of God in the hadith "God is beautiful and loves beauty". Zayn is beauty added, ornament rather than essence. Three roots, three different claims.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ج-م-لjamal, beauty as wholeness and proportion.
  • ح-س-نhusn, beauty and goodness in the same word.
  • ز-ي-نzayn, beauty as adornment, what is added.
