What Muslim names meaning noble have in common

Karam is the key word, and it will not separate nobility from generosity. To be karim is to give, and the Quran calls itself al-Quran al-Karim. The tradition is explicit that this nobility is not inherited: the most karim among you is the most God-conscious. Sharaf is elevation, high standing. Nubl is nobility of character rather than of station.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ك-ر-مkaram, nobility as generosity, the two inseparable.
  • ش-ر-فsharaf, honour as elevation, high standing.
  • ن-ب-لnubl, nobility of character.
  • ع-ل-وulu, height, from which al-Ali, the Most High.

How many Muslim boy names mean noble?
AsrNaam lists 46. Across boys and girls together, 107 researched names carry this meaning, 46 for boys and 61 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for noble?
Karam, from the root ك-ر-م: nobility as generosity, the two inseparable. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim boy name meaning noble is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Zaviyar, Luay and Ali lead this theme. The settled meaning of Zaviyar is: Brave and noble, the number one trending Muslim boy name in Pakistan 2025-2026.
Are there Quranic names meaning noble?
Yes. 1 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Ali. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning noble say in Ilm ul Huroof?
