What Muslim names meaning mercy have in common

Rahmah, mercy, comes from rahim, the womb. Every chapter of the Quran but one opens by naming God ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, and the tradition reads the shared root as deliberate: mercy that shelters and sustains before anything is earned. Lutf is kindness so fine it is not noticed while it works. Hilm is mercy as restraint, the choice not to act on a right you hold.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ر-ح-مrahmah, mercy, from the same root as the womb.
  • ل-ط-فlutf, subtle kindness, care too fine to see working.
  • ح-ل-مhilm, forbearance, mercy expressed as restraint.

Questions about Muslim names meaning mercy

How many Muslim boy names mean mercy?
AsrNaam lists 36. Across boys and girls together, 81 researched names carry this meaning, 36 for boys and 45 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for mercy?
Rahmah, from the root ر-ح-م: mercy, from the same root as the womb. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim boy name meaning mercy is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Ashfaq, Rafiq and Rahim lead this theme. The settled meaning of Ashfaq is: Most compassionate friend, kindest companion.
Are there Quranic names meaning mercy?
Yes. 1 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Abdulrahman. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning mercy say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Ain (ع), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
