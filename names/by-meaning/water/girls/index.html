What Muslim names meaning water have in common

Ghayth is the word for rain that arrives when it is needed, and its root also means to come to the aid of someone: the rain and the rescue are the same act. Arabic keeps it separate from matar, ordinary rainfall, which the Quran often uses for punishment. In a language shaped by desert, a name meaning water is a name meaning relief, and the choice between these roots is not decorative.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • غ-ي-ثghayth, rain that relieves, the same root as rescue.
  • ن-ه-رnahr, river.
  • ب-ح-رbahr, sea, and by extension anything vast.

Questions about Muslim names meaning water

How many Muslim girl names mean water?
AsrNaam lists 35. Across boys and girls together, 49 researched names carry this meaning, 14 for boys and 35 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for water?
Ghayth, from the root غ-ي-ث: rain that relieves, the same root as rescue. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim girl name meaning water is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Deniz, Kausar and Sindhu lead this theme. The settled meaning of Deniz is: Sea, ocean.
Are there Quranic names meaning water?
Yes. 2 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Kawthar and Tasnim. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning water say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Dal (د), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
