What Muslim names meaning strength have in common

Strength in Arabic naming is rarely muscle. Izz is the strength of a thing that cannot be humiliated, which is why al-Aziz is a divine name and why Aziz also means beloved and precious: what is dear is what cannot be taken. Quwwah is capacity, the power to do. Shidda is firmness under pressure. A name tells you which kind it means.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ق-و-يquwwah, strength as capacity, the power to act.
  • ع-ز-زizz, strength as unassailability, honour that cannot be taken.
  • ش-د-دshidda, strength as firmness, what does not give way.

Questions about Muslim names meaning strength

How many Muslim baby names mean strength?
AsrNaam lists 59. Across boys and girls together, 59 researched names carry this meaning, 51 for boys and 8 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for strength?
Quwwah, from the root ق-و-ي: strength as capacity, the power to act. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim baby name meaning strength is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Sameena, Hamza and Kenan lead this theme. The settled meaning of Sameena is: The strong, the full one.
Are there Quranic names meaning strength?
Yes. 2 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Hamza and Zubair. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning strength say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Ain (ع), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
