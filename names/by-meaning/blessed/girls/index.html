What Muslim names meaning blessed have in common

Barakah is the central word, and its root also gives baraka, a camel kneeling to rest. The image is of a good that settles rather than passes through: not a large amount, but an amount that lasts and increases where it is. Saad is fortune, the turn of circumstance. Yumn is blessing tied to the right side, which is why Ayman means both right-handed and blessed.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • ب-ر-كbarakah, blessing as increase that settles and stays.
  • س-ع-دsaad, good fortune, felicity.
  • ي-م-نyumn, blessing associated with the right hand.

Questions about Muslim names meaning blessed

How many Muslim girl names mean blessed?
AsrNaam lists 24. Across boys and girls together, 49 researched names carry this meaning, 25 for boys and 24 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for blessed?
Barakah, from the root ب-ر-ك: blessing as increase that settles and stays. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim girl name meaning blessed is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Yumna, Ayaana and Cham lead this theme. The settled meaning of Yumna is: Blessed, fortunate, the right-handed one, the woman of divine blessing.

The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Meem (م), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
