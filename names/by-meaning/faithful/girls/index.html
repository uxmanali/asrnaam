What Muslim names meaning faith have in common

Iman, faith, shares its root with amn, safety, and amin, trustworthy. To believe and to be someone others can rely on are the same word in Arabic, which is why the Prophet was called al-Amin before he was called anything else. Taqwa is from a root meaning to shield: awareness of God as a guard on conduct. Abd, servant, is the most common element in Muslim names of all.

The classical roots this meaning is carried by:

  • أ-م-نiman, faith, safety and trustworthiness in one root.
  • ع-ب-دabd, servant, the root of worship.
  • ت-ق-يtaqwa, God-consciousness, from a root meaning to guard.

Questions about Muslim names meaning faith

How many Muslim girl names mean faith?
AsrNaam lists 25. Across boys and girls together, 63 researched names carry this meaning, 38 for boys and 25 for girls. Each was placed here by reading the name against the classical lexicons, not by matching a word.
What is the Arabic word for faith?
Iman, from the root أ-م-ن: faith, safety and trustworthiness in one root. Arabic carries more than one word for it, and the others are set out above, because they do not mean the same thing.
Which Muslim girl name meaning faith is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Aabida, Barirah and Sajdah lead this theme. The settled meaning of Aabida is: Devoted worshipper, she who gives her life to worship.
Are there Quranic names meaning faith?
Yes. 2 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Ikhlas and Iman. A name does not have to be Quranic to be Islamic, but many parents look for one that is.
What does a name meaning faith say in Ilm ul Huroof?
The reading runs on the Arabic letters, not the English spelling. Most names in this theme open with Hamza (أ), which carries its own element, abjad value and divine name. Open any name above for its full letter-by-letter reading. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
