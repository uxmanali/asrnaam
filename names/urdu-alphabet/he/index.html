Questions about names beginning with ہے

How many Muslim names start with ہے in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 45, 17 for boys and 28 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is ہے written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 1 different opening letters, most often H. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu ہے the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. Urdu writes the light h as ہ, with a distinct final form, where Arabic writes ه. Urdu also uses ھ, the two-eyed he, purely to aspirate the letter before it, which Arabic has no use for. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
Which name beginning with ہے is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Hana, Hazal and Hashim lead this letter. The settled meaning of Hana is: Happiness, bliss, top global Muslim girls name.
What does He mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads He as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
