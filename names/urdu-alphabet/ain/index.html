Questions about names beginning with عین

How many Muslim names start with عین in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 262, 179 for boys and 83 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is عین written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 6 different opening letters, most often A, U and O. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Which name beginning with عین is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Aisha, Ammar and Arafat lead this letter. The settled meaning of Aisha is: Living, lively, prosperous, Mother of the Believers, wife of the Prophet.
What does Ain mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Ain as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
