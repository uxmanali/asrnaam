Questions about names beginning with بے

How many Muslim names start with بے in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 112, 59 for boys and 53 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is بے written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 2 different opening letters, most often B and P. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Which name beginning with بے is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Budur, Bello and Basheer lead this letter. The settled meaning of Budur is: Full moons, she who contains the brilliance of many full moons.
What does Be mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Be as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
