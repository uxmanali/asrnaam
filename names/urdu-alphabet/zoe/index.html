Questions about names beginning with ظوے

How many Muslim names start with ظوے in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 5, 3 for boys and 2 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is ظوے written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 2 different opening letters, most often Z and D. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu ظوے the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. Arabic sounds this as a heavy th. Urdu sounds it z, which is how Urdu ends up with four letters that all say z. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
What does Zoe mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Zoe as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
