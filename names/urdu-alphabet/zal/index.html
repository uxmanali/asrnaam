Questions about names beginning with ذال

How many Muslim names start with ذال in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 12, 10 for boys and 2 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is ذال written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 2 different opening letters, most often D and Z. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu ذال the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. Arabic sounds this as the th of this. Urdu sounds it z, which is why one Arabic name can arrive in Urdu with a different sound and the same spelling. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
Which name beginning with ذال is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Zulfiqar, Zeeshan and Dhul Faqar lead this letter. The settled meaning of Zulfiqar is: Dhul Fiqar, the sword of Ali.
What does Zal mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Zal as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
