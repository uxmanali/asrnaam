Questions about names beginning with لام

How many Muslim names start with لام in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 55, 17 for boys and 38 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is لام written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 1 different opening letters, most often L. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Which name beginning with لام is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Luay, Lucas and Latif lead this letter. The settled meaning of Luay is: Shield, protector, the ancestor of the Quraysh, guardian of noble lineage.
What does Lam mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Lam as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
