The Urdu letter یے (Ye)

  • Letterی
  • Urdu nameیے (Ye)
  • Soundy, i, or ee
  • Names here51, 32 for boys and 19 for girls
  • Usually writtenY (50), J (1) in English
  • Letter readingIts element, abjad value and divine name

Urdu writes ی where Arabic writes ي. Arabic dots the final ya. Urdu does not, and writes ی. A name typed with the Arabic ي will still be read, but it is not how a South Asian family writes it.

Questions about names beginning with یے

How many Muslim names start with یے in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 51, 32 for boys and 19 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is یے written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 2 different opening letters, most often Y and J. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu یے the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. Arabic dots the final ya. Urdu does not, and writes ی. A name typed with the Arabic ي will still be read, but it is not how a South Asian family writes it. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
Which name beginning with یے is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Yusuf, Yahya and Yunus lead this letter. The settled meaning of Yusuf is: The prophet Joseph.
What does Ye mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Ye as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
