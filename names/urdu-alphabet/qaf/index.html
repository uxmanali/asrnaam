Questions about names beginning with قاف

How many Muslim names start with قاف in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 30, 21 for boys and 9 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is قاف written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 2 different opening letters, most often Q and K. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Which name beginning with قاف is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Qaboos, Qais and Kanat lead this letter. The settled meaning of Qaboos is: Qaboos, Handsome, Omani royalty name.
What does Qaf mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Qaf as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
