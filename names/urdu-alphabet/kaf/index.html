The Urdu letter کاف (Kaf)

  • Letterک
  • Urdu nameکاف (Kaf)
  • Soundk
  • Names here59, 31 for boys and 28 for girls
  • Usually writtenK (53), C (4), O (1), Q (1) in English
  • Letter readingIts element, abjad value and divine name

Urdu writes ک where Arabic writes ك. Arabic adds a small hamza-like stroke inside the loop. Urdu keeps the plain form and never adds it. The same letter, the same sound, two hands.

Questions about names beginning with کاف

How many Muslim names start with کاف in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 59, 31 for boys and 28 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is کاف written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 4 different opening letters, most often K, C and O. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu کاف the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. Arabic adds a small hamza-like stroke inside the loop. Urdu keeps the plain form and never adds it. The same letter, the same sound, two hands. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
Which name beginning with کاف is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Kulsoom, Cyrus and Kian lead this letter. The settled meaning of Kulsoom is: Full-cheeked.
What does Kaf mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Kaf as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
