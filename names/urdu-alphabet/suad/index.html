Questions about names beginning with صاد

How many Muslim names start with صاد in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 46, 21 for boys and 25 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is صاد written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 1 different opening letters, most often S. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Is the Urdu صاد the same as the Arabic letter?
They are the same letter historically, written differently. One of the three s letters in Urdu. In Arabic it is emphatic and quite distinct from seen. Both forms appear on this site: the Arabic spelling and the Urdu spelling are given separately on every name page.
Which name beginning with صاد is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Sabir, Sabiha and Salih lead this letter. The settled meaning of Sabir is: Patient, enduring.
What does Suad mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Suad as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
