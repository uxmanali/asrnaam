Questions about names beginning with جیم

How many Muslim names start with جیم in Urdu?
AsrNaam lists 75, 42 for boys and 33 for girls. Each carries its Urdu spelling, its Arabic spelling, its meaning and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
How is جیم written in English?
There is no single answer, and that is the honest position. The names on this page appear in English under 4 different opening letters, most often J, C and D. Urdu has no official romanisation, so families spell by ear and both spellings are correct.
Which name beginning with جیم is most popular?
By what visitors search for and open on this site, Jasmine, Jana and Jafar lead this letter. The settled meaning of Jasmine is: The jasmine flower.
What does Jeem mean in Ilm ul Huroof?
The Science of Letters reads Jeem as carrying an element, an abjad value and a link to one of the divine names. Its full reading is on the letter page, and every name on this page is read letter by letter on its own page. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
