Islamic boy names beginning with R

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

Questions about Muslim names starting with R

How many Muslim boy names start with R?
AsrNaam lists 52 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter R accounts for 145 researched names, 52 for boys and 93 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does R correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Ra (ر), so the reading of all 52 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin R is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim boy name starting with R is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Rashid, Rafiq and Ruslan lead the letter R. The settled meaning of Rashid is: Rightly-guided, mature in judgment. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are all names starting with R Islamic names?
Not automatically. A name becomes Islamic through its meaning and through the absence of any claim on what belongs to God alone, not through its spelling or its first letter. Every name listed here has been checked on both counts.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with R?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
