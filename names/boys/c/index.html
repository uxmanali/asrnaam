Arabic boy names beginning with C

The English letter C has no single Arabic counterpart. The 25 names on this page open with 4 different Arabic letters, and in the Science of Letters each of those carries a different quality, element and divine name. Two names that both look like C in English can begin very differently once they are written in Arabic.

  • جJeem opens 10 of them, among them Celal, Cafer, Cahit.
  • شSheen opens 8 of them, among them Chakib, Chafiq, Chabane.
  • چCha opens 4 of them, among them Chakar, Chalak, Chand.
  • عAin opens 2 of them, among them Cabdi, Cabdinasir.

Islamic boy names beginning with C

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

None of the names starting with this letter are Quranic in the strict sense, but each one has been checked for meaning and for the rule against divine-only titles before it was listed.

Questions about Muslim names starting with C

How many Muslim boy names start with C?
AsrNaam lists 25 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter C accounts for 50 researched names, 25 for boys and 25 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does C correspond to?
There is no single answer, which is what makes this letter interesting. Most often C stands for Jeem (ج), which opens 10 of these names. It can also stand for Sheen, Cha and Ain. Each of those Arabic letters has its own element and divine name in the Science of Letters, so two names that look alike in English can read very differently.
Which Muslim boy name starting with C is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Cyrus, Chakib and Celal lead the letter C. The settled meaning of Cyrus is: Cyrus, Dhul-Qarnayn in some traditions, the just Persian king. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are all names starting with C Islamic names?
Not automatically. A name becomes Islamic through its meaning and through the absence of any claim on what belongs to God alone, not through its spelling or its first letter. Every name listed here has been checked on both counts.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with C?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
