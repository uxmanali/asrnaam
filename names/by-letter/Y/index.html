Islamic baby names beginning with Y

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

Questions about Muslim names starting with Y

How many Muslim baby names start with Y?
AsrNaam lists 50 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter Y accounts for 50 researched names, 31 for boys and 19 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does Y correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Ya (ي), so the reading of all 50 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin Y is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim baby name starting with Y is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Yusuf, Yahya and Yunus lead the letter Y. The settled meaning of Yusuf is: The prophet Joseph. Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with Y?
Yes. 4 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Yahya, Yaqub and Yunus. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with Y?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
