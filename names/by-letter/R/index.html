Arabic baby names beginning with R

Islamic baby names beginning with R

Islamic tradition asks two things of a name: that its meaning be good, and that it not claim for a child what belongs to God alone. Every name listed on this page is checked against both, which is why a pleasant-sounding name with an unhappy meaning does not appear here.

Questions about Muslim names starting with R

How many Muslim baby names start with R?
AsrNaam lists 145 of them. Across boys and girls together the letter R accounts for 145 researched names, 52 for boys and 93 for girls. Every one has its Arabic spelling, its meaning, and its letter-by-letter Ilm ul Huroof reading.
What Arabic letter does R correspond to?
One letter only. Every name here is written in Arabic beginning with Ra (ر), so the reading of all 145 of them starts from that letter: its element, its abjad value and the divine name attached to it. The Latin R is only how the name is transliterated.
Which Muslim baby name starting with R is most popular?
By what visitors actually search for and open on this site, Rabia, Rawda and Rashid lead the letter R. The settled meaning of Rabia is: Spring (the season). Popularity is worth knowing, but the tradition weighs meaning first.
Are there Quranic names beginning with R?
Yes. 2 of the names on this page appear in the Quran or belong to a figure named in it, among them Rahma and Rahmah. A Quranic name is not required for a name to be Islamic, but many parents look for one.
What is the Ilm ul Huroof reading of a name starting with R?
Open any name on this page and you will find it: the name broken into its Arabic letters, each letter with its element, its abjad value and the divine name it is associated with, then a reading of the whole. It is a tradition for reflection, not for prediction.
